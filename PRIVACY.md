# Privacy policy

*Super Dungeon Crawl, Android app `com.superdungeoncrawl.app`. Last updated
2026-11-22.*

Super Dungeon Crawl does not collect, store off the device, share or sell any
personal or usage data.

- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** The app contains no
  advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code.
- **No network use beyond the device.** The game runs entirely on your phone.
  The app's only network connection is to its own game server on the
  device's loopback address (127.0.0.1); it never connects to the internet.
- **Your saves stay on your device.** Saved games, options and morgue files
  are stored in the app's private storage. They leave the device only when
  you export a save yourself (Tools > Saves), to a location you choose.
- **Permissions.** `INTERNET` is used only for the loopback connection
  described above; `VIBRATE` gives haptic feedback during play.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/vimuston/super-dungeon-crawl/issues>.
